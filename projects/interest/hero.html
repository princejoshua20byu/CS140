My Hero - Iron Man

Interests Hero

My favorite Marvel hero is Iron Man. His real name is Tony Stark. He is strong, smart, and brave. He can solve many problems by himself. Like me, I always want to solve my own problems first before asking help, but sometimes I need a rescue before I solve my problems.

People Helped him:

Howard Stark
Howard Stark was Tony's father. He taught Tony about science, technology, and how to be successful.
Obadiah Stane
Obadiah Stane was Tony's father's business partner. His betrayal helped Tony understand that he needed to protect himself and other people.
Pepper Potts
Pepper Potts was Tony's assistant and close friend. She supported Tony and helped him become a better and more responsible person.
