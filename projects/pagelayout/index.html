Welcome to Halloween

Halloween is a fun holiday for many people. People decorate their houses with pumpkins, ghosts, and scary things. I like seeing the decorations and different costumes during Halloween.

On Halloween, children can go trick-or-treating with their friends and family. They wear costumes and collect candy from different houses. People can also watch scary movies, go to parties, and make pumpkin decorations.
