Welcome to Halloween

Halloween is a happy holiday here in Hawaii. Most of the people here are decorating their houses with pumpkins, ghosts, and scary things. The first time I saw it is like Christmas in the Philippines, they are decorating in advance.

Halloween day, children can go walk around and go trick or treating with their friends and family. They are wearing costumes and collect candy from different neighbors. People sometimes watch scary movies, go to friends' parties, and make pumpkin decorations.
