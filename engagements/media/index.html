1. What are the positives and negatives of hosting your videos on YouTube?

The positive side is that YouTube is free, easy to use, and can store large videos. The negative side is that it needs internet, may show ads, and you have less control over your videos.

2. What is the most useful thing you can do with a transform?

The most useful thing I can do with a transform is move, rotate, or resize an image or box on my webpage.

3. What is the difference between a transform and a transition?

A transform changes how an element looks or its position. A transition makes the change happen smoothly instead of suddenly.
