What kind of photos are good to encode using JPEG?

JPEG is good for photos with many colors, like pictures of people, nature, and places.

What kind of photos are good to encode using GIF?

GIF is good for simple images with few colors, drawings, logos, and simple animations.

What is unique about the PNG format?

PNG can have a transparent background. It also keeps good image quality. You can make logos, icons and buttons.

How do you resize your photo to the size it should be displayed?

Use photo editing software to change the photo's width and height to the size you need.

Why should you resize photos using photo editing software instead of resizing it using CSS?

Resizing with photo editing software makes the file smaller and helps the webpage load faster.
