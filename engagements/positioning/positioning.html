Positioning

Questions and Answers

1. What is normal flow in HTML?

Normal flow is the normal order of elements on a webpage.

2. What does float do? Why clear the float?

Float moves a picture so text wraps around it. Clear makes content start below the picture.

3. What do absolute, fixed, and relative do?

Relative moves a box but keeps its space. Absolute positions a box. Fixed stays in the same place on the screen.

4. How can you make navigation buttons for phones?

Use display: block, width: 100%, and padding to make links easier to tap.
